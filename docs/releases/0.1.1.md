# As The Crow Flies 0.1.1

Panel sizing and docking improvements.

- Start with a narrower default panel that takes up less screen space.
- Resize the panel in every view, including active directions, and keep your chosen size.
- Drag the collapsed edge tab up or down; its position is remembered.
- Keep the panel above most other interface elements with High layering by default, or select a lower layer in settings.
- Avoid stuck dragging or resizing when changing profiles, locking the panel or changing display settings.

Reload the UI after updating.
